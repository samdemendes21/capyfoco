Frequently asked questions

Is CapyFoco free?

Yes. The app is free with ads. Premium (optional) removes ads and unlocks Protected Mode, silenced notifications while you focus and 3 streak shields per month.

Do I need an account?

No. Your data stays on your phone. You can sync it to your own Google Drive if you want.

Does it work for exams?

Yes. Add your exam date, follow the countdown and get reminders 7, 3 and 1 day before.

How is CapyFoco different from Forest?

In Forest you grow trees. In CapyFoco you look after a capybara that grows as you study, with daily streaks, goals and exam countdowns in one app.

Is there an iPhone version?

Android only for now. An iPhone version is planned.
